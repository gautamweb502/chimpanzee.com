About The Species

More than just
a wild animal.

Chimpanzees are highly intelligent primates known for their complex social groups, communication and problem-solving abilities.

They live mainly in tropical forests and woodlands of Africa and spend much of their lives in social communities.

98% DNA similarity with humans
50+ Years possible lifespan
Africa Natural home
Social Community living

Chimpanzee facts.

A few fascinating facts about one of our closest living animal relatives.

🧠

Highly Intelligent

Chimpanzees can use tools and solve problems, demonstrating impressive cognitive abilities.

🌳

Forest Dwellers

They spend time both in trees and on the ground, depending on their environment and activities.

👥

Social Animals

Chimpanzees live in communities and maintain complex social relationships.
